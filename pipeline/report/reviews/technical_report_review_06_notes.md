## Evidence and remaining checks

The current calibration has five base seed pairs and 65 runs per culling state.
The displayed projections use 450 continuous-history runs across five climate
models, two pathways and nine culling levels. Full calibration ranges are not
confidence intervals; projection bands summarise the scenario ensemble.

These are calibration/diagnostic comparisons and exploratory projections.
They do not complete independent validation, the historical program-impact
assessment or an operational fifth-wave suppression recommendation. The 2026
population overshoot and outside larval supply remain important uncertainties.
Population/coral summaries cover five displayed reefs, while removals cover
the whole domain.

The existing author checks remain in the draft: connectivity attribution and
fine-scale network status, frozen software release, completion/coverage of the
full historical assessment, and agreed operational scenarios. Publication
permissions for the derived SALAD/cull material remain to be confirmed as
specified in the source metadata. They do not prevent this local review.
