- Annual full-coverage surveys including deferred sites, pre-closure checks and
  checks triggered by new COTS evidence are described as fallback options for
  field assessment. All follow-up effort must be included in seasonal accounting.
- The text distinguishes recovery of false negatives from new populations found
  on later visits. Sample audits during the initial visit could assess initial
  false negatives. Later coverage does not establish a negligible initial omission
  rate or guarantee detection/coral protection during a delay.

The combined ranking searches 66.6% of sites at 80% retention and 80.1% at 90%.
Those targets allow 20% and 10% of observed high-density dives to be outside
the initial selection. They remain options to assess, rather than agreed
operational stopping rules. Program agreement on omission tolerance, maximum
delay and the field design remains outstanding. No annual vessel-time saving
or measured recovery rate has been added.

Low-density avoidance refers to positive CPUE below 0.02; ghost avoidance refers
to dives with no COTS. Dives from 0.02 to 0.04 inclusive remain intermediate,
and the retained high-density outcome is strictly above 0.04. Table 3's columns
are percentages of designated sites or of each named dive class, respectively.
